Why I Like It

Cabaret has had numerous shows done, each one expanding on it a bit further. With different interpretations on the Emcee, the theme of it changes as well. However, the vital lesson of how politics affect everyone remains the same throughout. The musical itself has many fun songs, as well as serious songs with heavy themes. The characters are well written and have depths and layers to them that are only excentuated by the story they happen to be in. It is a painfully true story that warn so strongly and blatantly against facsism. It is so easily applied to politics today in so many different places that it is astounding. It's a timeless story that warns of fascism through such amazing artistic expression and symbolism, that it's truly marvelous.
