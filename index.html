Cabaret

Plot and Synopsis

Cabaret Motion Picture, mmxcpiny, flickr

The musical Cabaret first opened in 1951 and the movie was released in 1972. It takes place in 1930s Berlin, and follows the story of a writer, named Cliff Bradshaw, who is traveling for inspiration on his novel. Along the way, he meets Sally Bowles at a nightclub, called the Kit Kat Klub. From there, they started living together and become aquaintances with his landlord and other people who are living in her rooms. The story takes a turn as the pressence of Nazis, many of which are compatriots of the landlady and her soon to be husband, become more and more evident.

The story of Cabaret has lasted for decades and serves as one of the best political commentary musicals to date. It uses its display of flashing light, partying, and fun to mask the political climate it's currently in. It gives a great warning of what can transpire if politics are left unaccounted for. Nobody can say that "it's just politics". It also teaches that politics affect everyone, as (almost) every character suffers in the end.
