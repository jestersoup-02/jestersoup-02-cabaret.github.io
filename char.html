Important Characters in Cabaret

The Emcee:

The Emcee is essentially the narrarator of Cabaret, and has no affect on the characters. He's never mentioned by any other character, except for Cliff Bradshaw in the final song, "Finale". Because there is no need for him to interact with other characters, he himself is open to interpretation on what he is. He can be interpretted as either a mistaken victim, falling for Nazi propaganda, ignoring the state of the world until it's too late, or a represantation of a Nazi at the time. Because he can be interpreted in multiple ways, there is no set ending, letting the audience see what would happen to him, depending on his interpretation.


Cliff Bradshaw:

The show follows Cliff Bradshaw as he travels through Berlin and meets the rest of the cast. He is a struggling writer, possibly bisexual, and a good man through his morals and compassion for others. As the story progresses, he becomes more and more uncomfortably aware of the disturbing political climate as the rise of Nazis continues to grow. As he is sharing a room with Sally Bowles, he is conflicted on how to move on with his life. She, herself, is both pregnant with an unknown man's child, and continues to choose ignorace over the politics of the country she lives in. Ultimately, Cliff decides to leave Berlin and return to America, stating at the train station that "it was the end of the world".


Sally Bowles:

Sally Bowles is a flirtatious and naive showgirl at the Kit Kat Klub. She's often more focused on appearing mysterious and spontaneous, rather than her true character. Before meeting Cliff, she would sleep with men for a room, which would usually last for a week. She never takes things seriously, which has devastating effects on her mental state, as the overwhelming present hits her hard. As she comes to the realization of the present, she begins thinking about the future, of what's to come.


Ernst Ludwig

Ernst Ludwig is an early acquantance of Cliff and helps him get money to stay in his room. However, it's later revealed that he's a Nazi and was smuggling money for the Nazi Party. He himself as a character isn't very important, but the reveal of him being a Nazi very clearly sets and changes the tone for the rest of the musical.
