Noteable Actors

Given the context and value of Cabaret, finding the right actors is imperative for both a well-done show and a respectful depiction.

Many popular and well performed Emcees were played by:

  • Alan Cumming
  • Joel Grey
  • Eddie Redmayne
  • Orville Peck
  • Reeve Carney, who was also the original Orpheus in Broadway's prodcution of Hadestown

Different actors who have played Sally Bowles include:

  • Jane Horrocks
  • Liza Minnelli
  • Natasha Richardson
  • Amy Lennox

Different actors that played Cliff Bradshaw include:

  • John Benjamin Hickey
  • Boyd Gaines
